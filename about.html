Home • Our Heritage

Fifteen Years of Wood & Fabric Artistry

Founded in Kolkata's vibrant Topsia artisanal furniture hub, ZH Unique Furniture was established with a singular mission: to liberate homeowners from mass-produced, flimsy furniture by delivering bespoke luxury seating directly from the workshop.

Artisan Precision
Generational Technique

Built by Hand, Engineered for Life

At our Topsia workshop, every sofa starts with raw timber logs. Our senior ustaads (master craftsmen) hand-plane every wooden joint, string high-resilience S-spring suspension cords by feel, and meticulously align button tufts by hand.

Because we manufacture our own frames and foam cushions under one roof, we eliminate retail distribution markups, high-street showroom rents, and imported middleman commissions. You receive bespoke furniture built with materials twice as durable as retail alternatives at honest direct-from-maker pricing.
