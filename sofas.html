Material Specifications

The Anatomy of a ZH Unique Sofa

We never compromise on hidden internal framing. Compare our internal engineering with mass-market factory sofas.

1. Kiln-Dried Hardwood Core

We use thick-section Sal and Teak hardwood timber, air-seasoned and chemical-pressure treated to eliminate warping, splitting, and pest infestation.

2. Heavy-Duty Steel Suspension

High-tensile carbon steel S-springs interconnected with nylon-coated silent clips and cross-tension rubber webbing to eliminate bounce noise and sagging.

3. Orthopedic Multi-Density Padding

40-density HR base foam layered with 32-density supersoft top quilting and 150 GSM virgin polyester thermal wadding for long-lasting ergonomic shape retention.
