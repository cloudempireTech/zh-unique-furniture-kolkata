Architectural Excellence

The Standard of Bespoke Comfort

We bridge high-end architectural aesthetics with artisanal hand joinery, producing heirloom seating that outlasts retail alternatives.

Solid Sal & Teak Kiln-Dried Framework

Every frame is built from 100% termite-treated seasoned timber, assembled with mortise-and-tenon joints and corner-blocked bracing. Built to bear high dynamic loads without sagging, squeaking, or structural fatigue for over two decades.

40-Density Orthopedic Foam

Layered high-resilience foam core engineered to provide restorative lumbar support and luxurious sink-in plushness with zero bottoming out.

450+ Curated Fabrics & Leathers

Choose from stain-resistant hydrophobic velvet, breathable textured linens, and genuine top-grain or matte leatherette swatch books.

True Dimension Customization

Standard retail sofas rarely fit tricky alcoves or compact urban Kolkata apartments. We manufacture precisely to your floor plan dimensions down to the inch, ensuring seamless room flow and balanced proportions.
